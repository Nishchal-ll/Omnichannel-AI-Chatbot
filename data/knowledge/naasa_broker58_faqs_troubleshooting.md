# Naasa Securities (Broker 58) - FAQs & Troubleshooting

## How to Reset TMS 58 Password
If you have forgotten your password or your account is locked:
1. Visit https://tms58.nepsetms.com.np/.
2. Click on the "Forgot Password" link on the login page.
3. Enter your Client Code and registered Email Address.
4. An automated password reset link will be sent to your registered email address.
5. If the reset link does not arrive within 10 minutes or your account is disabled due to invalid attempts, email support@naasasecurities.com.np with your Client Code, Full Name, and a photo of your citizenship certificate.

## How to Request a Collateral Refund
1. Log into your TMS account at https://tms58.nepsetms.com.np/.
2. Check that you do not have any pending purchase settlements or unfulfilled open buy orders.
3. Navigate to "Fund Management" -> "Collateral Management" -> "Refund Collateral".
4. Enter the amount you wish to withdraw and submit the refund request.
5. Naasa Securities accounts department approves and transfers the amount to your registered bank account within 24 business hours.

## What Happens if I Miss the EDIS Deadline?
- If shares are not transferred via MeroShare EDIS by T+2 settlement morning:
- The clearing house (CDSC) initiates a "Close-out".
- As per SEBON regulations, a 20% close-out penalty of the gross sales amount is deducted from the seller and credited to the buyer.
- Always calculate WACC and transfer shares promptly through MeroShare after selling.

## Trading for Non-Resident Nepalese (NRN)
- Non-Resident Nepalese (NRN) and Nepalese working abroad can open TMS trading accounts online with Naasa Securities.
- Required documents: Valid Nepali Passport, valid foreign visa or work permit, NRN ID card (if applicable), Nepalese citizenship certificate, and an NRN convertible bank account in Nepal.

## Bank Account or Contact Details Update
- To change or update your bank account or registered mobile number in TMS:
- Send a formal request email to support@naasasecurities.com.np with an attached scanned copy of your citizenship certificate and a cancelled cheque of your new bank account.
