# Naasa Securities (Broker 58) - Brokerage Fees, Commission & Government Taxes

## SEBON Approved Brokerage Commission Slabs
As prescribed by the Securities Board of Nepal (SEBON), brokerage commission on equity share transactions is calculated on a tiered slab structure based on total transaction volume per company:
- Slab 1 (Up to NPR 50,000): 0.40% commission.
- Slab 2 (NPR 50,001 to NPR 500,000): 0.37% commission.
- Slab 3 (NPR 500,001 to NPR 2,000,000): 0.34% commission.
- Slab 4 (NPR 2,000,001 to NPR 10,000,000): 0.30% commission.
- Slab 5 (Above NPR 10,000,000): 0.24% commission.

## Government and Regulatory Fees
- SEBON Regulatory Fee: 0.015% of the total transaction amount (applicable to both buy and sell orders).
- Depository Participant (DP) Transaction Fee: Fixed fee of NPR 25 per company (scrip) deducted only when selling shares.
- NEPSE Regulatory Fee: Built inside the standard statutory commission sharing.

## Capital Gains Tax (CGT) on Share Profits
Applicable strictly on the net profit earned upon selling shares (calculated using WACC):
- Individual Resident (Short-Term: shares held for less than 365 days): 7.5% CGT.
- Individual Resident (Long-Term: shares held for 365 days or more): 5.0% CGT.
- Institutional Resident: 10.0% CGT.
- Non-Resident (Individual or Institutional): 25.0% CGT.

## Demat and MeroShare Renewal Charges
- Demat Annual Maintenance Charge (AMC): NPR 100 per financial year (payable before Ashadh end).
- MeroShare Annual Renewal Fee: NPR 50 per year.
- Renewals can be paid online via eSewa, Khalti, or ConnectIPS by searching for "Naasa Securities DP".
