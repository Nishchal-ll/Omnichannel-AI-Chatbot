# Naasa Securities Co. Ltd. (Broker No. 58) - Corporate Overview & Contacts

## About Naasa Securities
Naasa Securities Co. Ltd. is one of the leading and premier stock brokerage firms in Nepal, operating as registered Member No. 58 of the Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE). Naasa Securities is licensed and regulated by the Securities Board of Nepal (SEBON) to provide stock brokering, depository participant (DP), and financial market services.
