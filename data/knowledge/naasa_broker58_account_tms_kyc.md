# Naasa Securities (Broker 58) - Account Opening, KYC & TMS 58

## NEPSE TMS 58 Portal
- Official Trade Management System (TMS) Portal URL: https://tms58.nepsetms.com.np/
- Clients execute online buy and sell orders, view order books, check collateral, and manage settlements through TMS 58.

## Online KYC Registration Process
1. Visit https://tms58.nepsetms.com.np/ and click on "New Registration" / "Register".
2. Select Client Type: Individual, Institutional, or Non-Resident Nepalese (NRN).
3. Fill in General Details: Full name, mobile number, email address, date of birth, and permanent/current address.
4. Fill in Bank Details: Bank name, account number, and branch name (must match client's name for automatic payouts).
5. Fill in Depository Details: 16-digit Beneficiary Owner Identification Number (BOID / Demat Number).
6. Upload Mandatory Documents in JPG, PNG, or PDF format.
7. Submit the application and note the reference code.

## Mandatory Documents for Account Opening
- Valid Nepali Citizenship Certificate (both front and back copies).
- Recent passport-sized color photograph.
- Client's signature specimen on plain white paper.
- Location map / thumbprints (as requested on the KYC form).
- Cancelled bank cheque or bank account statement showing account number and client name clearly.
- Permanent Account Number (PAN) Card (mandatory if annual stock transactions exceed NPR 500,000, recommended for all).
- Minor accounts: Birth Certificate of minor and Citizenship certificate of legal guardian.

## Demat and MeroShare Services
- Depository Participant (DP) Name: Naasa Securities Co. Ltd.
- DP ID: 13900.
- Clients can open both Demat and MeroShare accounts simultaneously through Naasa Securities.
- MeroShare portal: https://meroshare.cdsc.com.np/ allows online IPO application, portfolio tracking, and Electronic Transfer of Shares (EDIS).

## Account Verification Timeline
- Account review and verification typically takes 24 to 48 working hours after online KYC submission.
- Once approved, the client receives an automated email from NEPSE TMS 58 containing the username, default password, and a password reset link.
