## 2. Hassle-Free Bluebook Ownership Transfer (नामसारी)
- **100% Dealership-Handled Transfer**: All Bluebook ownership transfer (नामसारी) procedures are managed by our legal liaisons at Yatayat Karyalaya (Ekantakuna, Lalitpur and Gurjudhara, Kathmandu).
- **Timeframe**: Transfer completed within 24 to 48 working hours.
- **Vehicle Clearance**: Every car has up-to-date road tax paid (2081/82 pass), zero pending traffic violations, and clean police verification.

## 3. Instant Vehicle Exchange & Trade-in (साटासाट)
- **Fast 20-Minute Valuation**: Bring your old two-wheeler (motorcycle/scooter) or four-wheeler (any brand or lot number) for free on-the-spot technical inspection.
- **Highest Market Value**: We offer verified market valuation. The valuation amount is deducted directly from your chosen reconditioned vehicle's down payment.
- **Same-Day Delivery**: Drive in with your old vehicle, complete exchange paperwork, and drive out with your certified car the same day.

## 4. Bank Financing and Easy EMI Facilities
- **Financing Coverage**: Up to 70% bank financing on eligible private vehicles through partner commercial banks (NIC Asia Bank, Nabil Bank, Global IME Bank, Kumari Bank).
- **Down Payment**: Minimum 30% to 40% initial down payment.
- **Loan Tenure & Interest**: 3 to 7 years repayment tenure with competitive auto loan interest rates.
- **Quick Pre-Approval**: Loan pre-approval processed within 3 business days with basic citizenship, income source, and tax clearance documents.

## 5. Certification, Quality Guarantee, and Warranty
- **120-Point Mechanical Inspection**: Chassis frame integrity, engine compression, suspension, electrical systems, and verified genuine odometer reading (no meter tampering).
- **Warranty**: 6-Month / 8,000 km warranty covering engine block and gearbox on all certified Grade 4.5+ vehicles.
- **After-Sales Service**: 1 complimentary complete servicing including engine oil change, oil filter replacement, and general tune-up at our certified service partner.
