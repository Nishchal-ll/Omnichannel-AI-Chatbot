# Lionel Messi: Career, Achievements, Statistics, and Biography

## Overview and Early Life
Lionel Andrés Messi, born on June 24, 1987, in Rosario, Santa Fe, Argentina, is widely regarded as one of the greatest football (soccer) players in the history of the sport. He plays primarily as a forward or attacking playmaker, known for his low center of gravity, extraordinary dribbling at high speed, precision passing, vision, and prolific finishing.
As a child in Rosario, Messi played for local club Grandoli (coached by his father) before joining Newell's Old Boys at age six. At age ten, he was diagnosed with a growth hormone deficiency (GHD). When local clubs could not fund his medical treatments, FC Barcelona scout Charly Rexach offered a trial and agreed to pay for his medical treatment, leading Messi and his family to move to Barcelona, Spain, in February 2001 to join FC Barcelona's famed youth academy, La Masia.

## Senior Professional Clubs Summary (Clubs Played)
Throughout his senior professional career, Lionel Messi has played for three clubs:
1. **FC Barcelona** (Spain, La Liga) — 2004 to 2021: 778 appearances, 672 goals, 303 assists, 35 trophies (including 10 La Liga titles and 4 UEFA Champions League titles).
2. **Paris Saint-Germain (PSG)** (France, Ligue 1) — 2021 to 2023: 75 appearances, 32 goals, 35 assists, 3 trophies (2 Ligue 1 titles, 1 Trophée des Champions).
3. **Inter Miami CF** (United States, Major League Soccer / MLS) — July 2023 to present: Won Leagues Cup 2023 and Supporters' Shield 2024.
Prior to his senior professional debut, Messi played youth football in Argentina for Grandoli and Newell's Old Boys before developing at Barcelona's La Masia.

## Career Trophies, Records, and MSN Summary
- **Total Career Trophies**: Lionel Messi has won over 45 official team trophies across his career, making him the most decorated footballer in association football history. This includes 35 trophies with FC Barcelona, 3 with PSG, 2 with Inter Miami CF, and major titles with Argentina (2022 World Cup, 2021 & 2024 Copa América, 2022 Finalissima, 2008 Olympic Gold).
- **Historic MSN Attacking Trio**: Between 2014 and 2017 at FC Barcelona, Lionel Messi, Luis Suárez, and Neymar Jr. formed the legendary attacking trio dubbed "MSN", winning the historic treble (La Liga, Copa del Rey, and UEFA Champions League) in 2014–2015.
- **Calendar Year 2012 World Record**: In 2012, Messi achieved the Guinness World Record for scoring 91 official goals in a single calendar year (79 for Barcelona, 12 for Argentina).

## FC Barcelona Career (2004–2021)
Messi spent 21 years with FC Barcelona, including 17 seasons with the senior first team, making his official first-team debut on October 16, 2004, at age 17 against Espanyol under manager Frank Rijkaard.
- **Sextuple Era (2008–2009)**: Under manager Pep Guardiola, Barcelona completed an unprecedented sextuple in 2009, winning La Liga, the Copa del Rey, the UEFA Champions League, the Supercopa de España, the UEFA Super Cup, and the FIFA Club World Cup. Messi was the centerpiece of the team, playing as a "false nine".
- **The MSN Treble (2014–2015)**: Under manager Luis Enrique, Messi formed the historic attacking trio with Luis Suárez and Neymar Jr. (dubbed "MSN"), winning the treble (La Liga, Copa del Rey, and UEFA Champions League) in the 2014–2015 season.
- **Barcelona Statistics & Trophies**: Messi scored an all-time club record 672 goals and registered 303 assists in 778 official appearances for FC Barcelona. He won 35 trophies with Barcelona: 10 La Liga titles, 4 UEFA Champions League titles (2006, 2009, 2011, 2015), 7 Copa del Rey trophies, 8 Supercopa de España, 3 UEFA Super Cups, and 3 FIFA Club World Cups. He wore jersey number 10 from 2008 until his departure in 2021.

## Paris Saint-Germain Era (2021–2023)
On August 10, 2021, after FC Barcelona was unable to renew his contract due to La Liga financial fair play regulations, Messi signed a two-year contract with French club Paris Saint-Germain (PSG).
- At PSG, Messi chose jersey number 30 (the number he wore during his senior debut at Barcelona) and reunited with Neymar Jr. alongside Kylian Mbappé.
- During his two seasons in France, Messi made 75 appearances in all competitions for PSG, scoring 32 goals and providing 35 assists.
- He won three trophies with PSG: two Ligue 1 titles (2021–22, 2022–23) and the 2022 Trophée des Champions. In the 2022–23 season, he finished as Ligue 1's top assist provider with 16 assists.

## Inter Miami CF and Major League Soccer (2023–Present)
On July 15, 2023, Messi officially signed with Major League Soccer (MLS) club Inter Miami CF in the United States, taking jersey number 10.
- **Leagues Cup 2023**: In August 2023, Messi led Inter Miami to their first-ever club trophy by winning the Leagues Cup 2023. Messi scored 10 goals in 7 tournament games, winning both the Top Scorer and Best Player awards.
- **Supporters' Shield 2024**: In October 2024, Messi captained Inter Miami to win the MLS Supporters' Shield with a record regular-season points total.
- Messi's arrival in MLS generated global attention, setting attendance and viewership records across stadiums in North America.

## Argentina National Team Career and International Titles
Messi represents the Argentina senior national team as captain and is the country's all-time leading goalscorer and most capped player:
- **Youth & Olympic Success**: Won the 2005 FIFA World Youth Championship (Under-20) in the Netherlands, winning the Golden Ball and Golden Shoe. He won the Olympic Gold Medal at the 2008 Beijing Olympics with the Argentina Under-23 team.
- **2021 Copa América**: In July 2021, Messi captained Argentina to victory in the Copa América, defeating Brazil 1–0 in the final at the Maracanã Stadium in Rio de Janeiro. This was Argentina's first major senior international trophy in 28 years. Messi was named Best Player and joint top goalscorer (4 goals, 5 assists).
- **2022 Finalissima**: On June 1, 2022, Argentina defeated European champions Italy 3–0 at Wembley Stadium to win the CONMEBOL-UEFA Cup of Champions (Finalissima). Messi was named Player of the Match.
- **2022 FIFA World Cup Champion (Qatar)**: In December 2022, Messi led Argentina to win the 2022 FIFA World Cup in Qatar. Messi scored 7 goals (scoring in the group stage, round of 16, quarter-final, semi-final, and twice in the final vs France) and provided 3 assists. Argentina won the final on penalties after a 3–3 draw. Messi was awarded the World Cup Golden Ball as the tournament's best player, becoming the first player in history to win the Golden Ball twice (2014 and 2022).
- **2024 Copa América**: In July 2024, Messi captained Argentina to retain the Copa América title in the United States, defeating Colombia 1–0 in the final.
- Overall, Messi has scored over 112 international goals in more than 190 appearances for Argentina.

## Individual Awards and Honors
Lionel Messi holds the most individual accolades in association football history:
- **Ballon d'Or (8 awards)**: Messi has won the prestigious Ballon d'Or a record 8 times: 2009, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2015, 2019, 2021, and 2023.
- **European Golden Shoe (6 awards)**: Top league goalscorer in Europe for 2009–10 (34 goals), 2011–12 (50 goals), 2012–13 (46 goals), 2016–17 (37 goals), 2017–18 (34 goals), and 2018–19 (36 goals).
- **FIFA World Player of the Year / The Best FIFA Men's Player**: Won in 2009, 2019, 2022, and 2023.
- **Laureus World Sportsman of the Year**: Won in 2020 (shared with Lewis Hamilton) and 2023, making him the only football player to win the award.
- **World Cup Golden Ball**: Won twice (2014 in Brazil and 2022 in Qatar).

## World Records and Career Statistics
- **Most Goals in a Calendar Year**: Guinness World Record for scoring 91 official goals in the year 2012 (79 for Barcelona, 12 for Argentina), surpassing Gerd Müller's previous record of 85 goals set in 1972.
- **Most Goals in a Single Club Season**: 73 goals in all competitions during the 2011–2012 season for FC Barcelona.
- **Most Goals in a Single La Liga Season**: 50 goals in the 2011–2012 season.
- **All-Time La Liga Top Scorer**: 474 goals in 520 La Liga appearances.
- **Most Assists in Football History**: Over 380 official career assists for club and country.
- **Most Trophies Won by Any Player**: Over 45 official team trophies, making him the most decorated footballer in history.

## Personal Life, Family, and Philanthropy
- **Family**: Messi married his childhood sweetheart Antonela Roccuzzo in 2017 in their hometown of Rosario. They have three sons: Thiago (born 2012), Mateo (born 2015), and Ciro (born 2018).
- **Philanthropy**: In 2007, he established the Leo Messi Foundation (Fundación Leo Messi), a charity supporting access to healthcare, education, and sports for vulnerable children worldwide.
- **UNICEF**: Messi has served as a UNICEF Goodwill Ambassador since March 2010, participating in campaigns for children's rights and education.
