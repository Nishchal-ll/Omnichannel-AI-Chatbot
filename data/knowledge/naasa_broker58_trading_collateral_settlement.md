# Naasa Securities (Broker 58) - Trading, Collateral & Settlement

## How to Place Buy and Sell Orders
- Log into https://tms58.nepsetms.com.np/ using your client code, password, and captcha.
- Navigate to "Order Management" -> "Buy/Sell".
- Enter the Stock Symbol (e.g. NABIL, NICA, SHIVM), Order Type (Limit or Market), Quantity (units), and Price per share.
- Submit the order. Trading hours are 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM, Sunday through Thursday.

## Collateral Management & Loading
- To place Buy orders, clients must maintain sufficient collateral in TMS 58.
- Loading Collateral via ConnectIPS:
  1. Log into TMS 58.
  2. Navigate to "Fund Management" -> "Collateral Management" -> "Load Collateral".
  3. Select Payment Gateway: ConnectIPS or Bank Transfer.
  4. Enter the amount to deposit and complete authentication.
  5. The collateral reflects instantly in the trading limit.
- Trading Limits: As per regulatory norms, cash collateral grants trading purchasing power.

## NEPSE T+2 Settlement Cycle
- NEPSE operates on a strict T+2 rolling settlement cycle.
- "T" is the trade execution day.
- "T+1": Clearing and settlement calculation day.
- "T+2": Final settlement day where shares are credited to the buyer's Demat and funds are credited to the seller's bank account.

## Electronic Transfer of Shares (EDIS) Procedure
- When selling shares, the seller must transfer the sold shares electronically using CDSC MeroShare EDIS:
  1. Log into MeroShare (https://meroshare.cdsc.com.np/).
  2. Go to "My Purchase Source" and calculate WACC (Weighted Average Cost of Capital) if not already calculated.
  3. Go to "My EDIS" -> "Transfer Shares".
  4. View the settlement ID for the shares sold through Broker 58.
  5. Select the sold shares and confirm the transfer before 7:00 PM on T+1 or before 10:00 AM on T+2 morning.
- Important Warning: Failure to transfer shares via EDIS leads to "Auction / Close-out", where the seller is penalized with a mandatory 20% fine of the total transaction amount as mandated by SEBON/NEPSE regulations.

## Fund Payout (Sales Payment)
- When a client sells shares, the net sales proceeds (after deducting brokerage commission, SEBON fee, DP fee, and applicable Capital Gains Tax) are deposited directly into the client's registered bank account on T+2 day.
- Clients can also request payout via TMS: "Fund Management" -> "Fund Payout".
